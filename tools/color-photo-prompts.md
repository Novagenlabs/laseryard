# Color product photos — image-model prompt pack (Nano Banana / GPT Image 2)

Every prompt reproduces the site's sample card design — the "Alex Carter" layout shown in the product page's preview — in one of the 11 stock colors. Each prompt is self-contained: copy one, paste it into Nano Banana (Gemini) or GPT Image 2, save the result under the filename above it.

**Thickness — read this first:** a real card is 0.8 mm, about credit-card thickness. Earlier drafts said "noticeably thick" and the models turned the card into a chunky slab. The prompts now say credit-card thickness explicitly: a single card must look thin with a crisp, straight-cut edge, and the fanned stack shot is what conveys "solid metal".

**Attach the reference image to every generation:** `tools/reference-card-design.png` (the design rendered on a silver card; `reference-card-design.svg` is the vector source). The prompts also spell the whole design out in words, so a generation still lands close without it — but the attachment is what makes it match.

**What the site uses:** only the fanned-stack shot, one per color, converted to WebP (1600 px wide, quality ~82) and saved as `public/images/products/colors/<id>.webp` — the product page picks it up at build time; a color without a file falls back to the generated preview. The hero prompts are kept below for other uses (ads, social). Generate landscape (1536 × 1024 / 3:2) at highest quality.

**Consistency:** generate `black.jpg` and `black-stack.jpg` first. For every later color, also attach the matching black result and append: "Keep exactly the same composition, camera angle and lighting as the attached black card image; only the card color changes."

**Reject and regenerate if:** any engraved line is misspelled or altered (check: AC · ALEX CARTER · FOUNDER & CEO · +1 415 000 0000 · alexcarter.com); the QR icon is missing or turned into a real QR code; the card looks thicker than a credit card, like a slab, or has a beveled edge; the background isn't pure white edge to edge; the finish looks glossy or plastic.

The color descriptions were sampled from the supplier's product photos: their "Deep Forest Green" is a muted sage, their gold a pale champagne, their pink a very pale blush.
